# Job offer sources by country

Starting points, not an exhaustive list. Sites change: if one is down or irrelevant, move on and say so. Always add **company career pages** (search "<company> careers/carrières/recrutement"), the **school/university career service or job board**, and **LinkedIn** (Jobs + posts from recruiters). For any country not listed, search "<country> official job portal", "<country> internship site", "<country> apprenticeship portal".

Useful search patterns (adapt language):
- `"<role>" alternance <city>` · `"<role>" internship <city> 2026` · `site:<board-domain> <role> <city>`
- Exact job title + synonyms; English titles too for international companies.

## France
- **France Travail** (francetravail.fr) — national public employment service, all contracts. Fetch its own results page (`https://candidat.francetravail.fr/offres/recherche?motsCles=<keywords>&lieux=<commune INSEE code, or département code + "D" like 44D>`) rather than offer links from a web search, which are often expired. Offer pages are `/offres/recherche/detail/<id>`.
- **La Bonne Alternance** (labonnealternance.apprentissage.beta.gouv.fr) — apprenticeships + companies likely to hire apprentices
- **1jeune1solution** (1jeune1solution.gouv.fr) — jobs, internships, apprenticeships for under-30s
- **Welcome to the Jungle** (welcometothejungle.com) — startups/SMEs, good company pages
- **Indeed** (fr.indeed.com), **LinkedIn**, **HelloWork** (hellowork.com), **APEC** (apec.fr, managers/graduates)
- **JobTeaser** (via the school), **Jobirl**, **StudentJob** (student jobs)
- **Choisir le service public** (choisirleservicepublic.gouv.fr) — public sector
- Apprenticeships: pay is a regulated % of the minimum wage depending on age/year; internships > 2 months must pay the legal minimum allowance ("gratification").

## Switzerland
- **job-room.ch** (official), **jobs.ch**, **jobup.ch** (French-speaking), **yousty.ch** (apprenticeships), **orientation.ch** (apprenticeship places)

## Canada / Québec

## Europe-wide / international / remote
- **EURES** (eures.europa.eu) — EU mobility, **Erasmus Intern** / **Erasmus+** traineeships, **EU Careers / Blue Book traineeship**
- Remote: **We Work Remotely**, **Remote OK**, **Remotive**, **LinkedIn (remote filter)**
- International orgs: UN Careers, OECD, World Bank YPP, ReliefWeb

## Red flags (any country)
- Asks for money (training fees, equipment, "file fees") or bank details before hiring
- Contact only via WhatsApp/Telegram, generic email (gmail etc.) for a "big company"
- No company name, no address, copy-pasted vague description
- Pay far above market for little work; immediate hire without interview
- Offer date > 2 months old with no recent activity → likely filled
