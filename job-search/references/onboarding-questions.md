# Onboarding question bank

Pick from these; don't ask them all. Translate into the user's language and adapt the options to their country (e.g. "BTS / BUT / Master" in France, "A-levels / Bachelor's" in the UK). Ask 3–5 per round. Items marked *(optional)* must be presented as optional.

## Round 1 — Search type & situation

1. **What are you looking for?** (multi-select)
   Apprenticeship / work-study (alternance) · Internship (stage) · Fixed-term contract (CDD) · Permanent contract (CDI) · Freelance / missions · Student / part-time job
2. **Where are you right now?**
   High school · In higher education · Recent graduate (< 2 years) · Working, want to change · Career change / retraining · Looking for work
3. **Your programme or last diploma:** name, speciality, school/university, year (e.g. "2nd year of a Bachelor in marketing at …"). For apprenticeships: is the school already confirmed, or do they need a school too?
4. **Experience so far** (short): internships, jobs, volunteering, freelance.

## Round 2 — Target

5. **Which role(s) do you want?** Free text. If unsure → propose 2–3 roles based on their programme and ask which appeal.
6. **Sectors you'd like** and **sectors to avoid** (e.g. ethics: defence, tobacco, fossil fuels…).
7. **Company size:** Startup · SME · Large company · Public sector / association · No preference.

## Round 3 — Dates

8. **When can you start?** ASAP · Specific month · Flexible
9. **Duration:** (internship: e.g. 2, 4–6 months; apprenticeship: 1, 2, 3 years; job: open)
10. **Work-study rhythm** (if apprenticeship): imposed by school? (e.g. 1 week school / 3 weeks company, 2 days / 3 days)
11. **For internships:** required by the programme? Is an internship agreement (convention de stage) needed?

## Round 4 — Location & mobility

12. **Home city / area.**
13. **Max commute or radius** (e.g. 30 min, 50 km).
14. **Remote work:** On-site · Hybrid · Full remote · Doesn't matter
15. **Open to moving?** No · Within region · Within country · Abroad (which countries?)
16. **Driving licence / vehicle?** *(optional)* — useful for offers outside city centres.

## Round 5 — Skills

17. **Technical / job skills** and **tools/software** they're comfortable with.
18. **Languages and level** (native, fluent, B2, basic…).
19. **1–3 projects or achievements they're proud of** (school project, personal project, results at a job). These fuel cover letters.
20. **Soft skills** colleagues/teachers would cite *(optional)*.

## Round 6 — Constraints & preferences

21. **Minimum pay / salary** *(optional)* — for apprenticeships/internships in some countries pay is regulated (sometimes by age and year); mention it briefly if relevant. Ask age only if it changes pay or eligibility, and keep it optional.
22. **Hours constraints** (no nights/weekends, part-time, caring duties) *(optional)*.
23. **Disability or workplace accommodations** *(optional, never required)* — only to target inclusive employers or relevant schemes if the user wishes. Do not ask for diagnosis details.
24. **Deal-breakers** — anything that rules an offer out.

## Round 7 — Documents & online presence

25. **Do you have a CV? A cover letter?** Up to date? (Offer to review it if they share it.)
26. **Links:** portfolio, LinkedIn, GitHub, personal site, Behance… *(optional)*

## Round 8 — Progress

27. **Already applied somewhere?** Which companies/roles, when, any answers? → goes into the tracking table.
28. **Tracking tool used?** None · Spreadsheet · Notion/Trello · Platform dashboards · Other. If none, propose the profile's tracking table.

## Tips

- Pre-fill from what the user already said; confirm instead of re-asking.
- After rounds 1–4, offer to start searching; continue the rest later or as needed (e.g. ask skills only when writing a cover letter).
- "I don't know" is a fine answer. Write it down and help them decide later.
